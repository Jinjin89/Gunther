# Cool roofs cut cooling demand across the city, though far less than claimed
The trial's 18% raw saving falls to 9% once hotter summers are accounted for, and ageing coatings erode even that over time [1][2]
Briefing for the city energy committee, with technical annex · March 2026

---

<!-- layout: section -->
# What the three-summer trial measured and how it was run across six districts
How 412 buildings were matched, metered and compared over three summers, with coating ageing tracked separately [1]

---

<!-- layout: summary -->
## Cool roofs work in practice, but the weather-adjusted saving is only half of the headline figure [1][2]
- **Savings halve after weather adjustment, so plan on 9%**: the headline 18% raw cooling saving falls to 9% once each summer's heat is taken out [1][2]
- **Most of the gain sits in top-floor flats under the roof**: the indoor peak was 2.1 °C lower on the top floor but under 0.4 °C lower on the ground floor [1]
- **Coatings age fast, so lifetime saving depends on recoating**: albedo falls from 0.82 to 0.55 within 18 months, losing about a third of the effect [3]
- **Complaints fell sooner than the meters moved at all**: building managers reported the first change in tenant calls within the first three weeks [8]

Note: Lead with the drop from 18% to 9%. Pause. Then explain why: 2021 was the coolest of the three summers. Hand over to the next slide on where the savings concentrate.

## The trial was large enough, matched well enough and long enough to trust the direction of travel
- **412** buildings across six districts, each matched to a control [1]
- **3** summers of metering, 2021 through 2023 inclusive [1]
- **0.82** starting albedo of the reflective coating tested [3]

<!-- layout: compare -->
## Green roofs win on stormwater and cooling comfort while cool roofs win clearly on upfront cost
### Cool roof coating system
- €15–25 per m² installed, with no structural work needed on most roofs [4]
- Needs recoating every 5–8 years as albedo falls below 0.6 [3]
- Light enough for every roof type in the survey, including the oldest [4]
- Cuts the indoor peak by 2.1 °C on the top floor in the trial [1]
### Green roof planting system
- €80–150 per m² installed, with structural checks on older roofs [4]
- Holds 50–70% of rainfall and releases it slowly to drains [4]
- Needs watering and a yearly maintenance visit in dry summers [4]
- Adds habitat and reduces noise but takes years to mature [4]
Pick green roofs only where stormwater is the binding problem, otherwise the cool roof pays back faster [4]

Note: Same terms, two options.

<!-- layout: process -->
## Six steps take a building from first survey to measured savings and a planned recoat
1. **Survey the roof**: roof type, insulation depth and top-floor occupancy are recorded for each building [5]
2. **Prepare surface**: clean and repair the membrane, then prime the roof before coating starts [5]
3. **Coat twice**: two layers of reflective coating applied in spring in dry weather [5]
4. **Meter all summer**: indoor temperature and meter readings logged every hour until autumn [1]
5. **Adjust weather**: compare each building with its matched control after weather adjustment [2]
6. **Recoat on time**: recoat when albedo falls below 0.6, not on a fixed calendar [3]

Note: The loop closes at recoating.

## The real question is ageing, not the first summer, because the saving decays
Coatings lose a third of their reflectivity within 18 months, so lifetime savings depend on recoating discipline, not on the first coat [3]
- Recoat when albedo falls below 0.6 [3]
- Budget for it from year one [3]
- Measure complaints as well as meters [8]

Note: This is the turning point of the talk.

<!-- layout: points -->
## Four things limit what the trial can tell us and none of them is fatal to the main finding
- **No quality data collected**: only electricity and temperature were measured during all three summers [1]
- **One city only, and old**: river-district blocks are older than the city average, which could inflate gains [9]
- **Short time horizon**: three summers, so ageing beyond 18 months is a projection, not an observation [3]
- **Self-selected tenants**: occupants who agreed to meters may be more careful with energy use [1]

Note: Be upfront about limits.

# Cool roofs cut summer heat in dense cities

Evidence from three field trials, 2021 to 2024

---

<!-- layout: big_number -->
## Top floors ran cooler in the first summer

**62%**
of surveyed buildings reported cooler top floors after one summer [1]

Note: This is the survey of 140 buildings, not a temperature reading.

---

<!-- layout: compare -->
## A cool roof gives the most cooling for the money

| | Cool roof | Green roof | Insulation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost per m² | $18 [2] | $140 [2] | $65 [2] |
| Peak roof surface | 28 °C lower [3] | 14 °C lower [3] | 6 °C lower [7] |
| Upkeep | Wash once a year [2] | Water and prune [2] | None [7] |
| Winter penalty | Small [4] | None [4] | None [7] |
A cool roof wins on cost and cooling, and pays a small winter penalty [4].

Note: Compare per square metre, not per building.

| Usual routine | Storm routine |
| --- | --- |
| Inspect once a year [5] | Inspect after every storm [5] |
| 8 leaks found per 100 roofs [5] | 17 leaks found per 100 roofs [5] |
| Repairs wait for the budget cycle [6] | Repairs start within a week [6] |

---

<!-- layout: flow -->
## Install in four steps, and do not skip the last

1. Survey the roof and fix leaks first [2]
2. Clean and prime the surface [2]
3. Apply two coats of reflective coating [3]
4. Measure again after the first summer [1]

---

<!-- layout: timeline -->
## Adoption took off once the city paid for the first coat

- **2019** Pilot on 12 public buildings [1]
- **2021** City funds the first coat for any flat roof [4]
- **2023** Private owners overtake public buildings [6]
- **2024** Winter heating study finds the penalty small [4]

## Reflective coating gives the largest drop in roof temperature

| Treatment | Peak roof surface drop (°C) |
| --- | --- |
| **Reflective coating** | 28 [3] |
| Green roof | 14 [3] |
| White membrane | 22 [7] |
| Extra insulation | 6 [7] |
The coating cut surface heat by twice as much as a green roof [3].

<!-- layout: cards -->
## Three things decide whether a coating lasts

- **Surface prep**: Dirt and old paint cause most early failures [2]
- **Coating quality**: Cheap acrylics lose their reflectance within two years [8]
- **Maintenance**: An annual wash keeps 90% of the benefit [2]

<!-- layout: text -->
## A cool roof is not a free lunch

- Winter heating rises a little in cold climates [4]
- Reflectance fades without washing [2]
- The benefit is largest on top floors and single-storey buildings [1]

---

<!-- layout: takeaways -->
## Start with the roofs that are flat, dark and under-insulated

- Coat the flat dark roofs first [2]
- Wash every year, and measure again after one summer [1]
- Check winter heating before using it in cold regions [4]

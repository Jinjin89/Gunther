## Literature table

The table of cool-roof studies is now close to complete. Each study has been entered with its city, its building type, the length of the observation period and its main results on indoor temperature and cooling energy. The group agreed that studies from cities with very different climates should be flagged and not mixed with the rest, and that a column should be added for whether results were adjusted for the weather. A few entries still lack the original data and will be marked as such.

## Council briefing

The group will compare cool-roof results across cities for a council briefing in June 2026. The briefing should be short, with no more than five slides or two pages, written for people who are not specialists, and should say plainly what is known and what is not. The group will start from the studies in the table and from the city's own trial, and will mention the main trade-offs, including winter heating and glare for neighbouring buildings. A draft is due in the second week of May so that there is time for comments from the environment office.

## Open question

Open question recorded: whether Okafor's weather adjustment should be used in the briefing. Arguments in favour were that the adjusted figure is the more defensible one and that other cities' results are better compared after adjustment. Arguments against were that the council has already seen the earlier number, so that a change would need an explanation, and that other studies in the table are not adjusted. No decision was taken; the question will be raised again at the next meeting after the table has been checked for how many studies adjust for the weather.

# Style: Bold
Mood: energetic, persuasive, modern. Good for strategy, product launches, pitches, talks that
must move people.

```css
.slide { --bg:#FFFFFF; --text:#111111; --muted:#5E5E5E; --panel:#F4F4F4; --line:#E2E2E2;
  --dominant:#FF4F2E; --accent:#111111; --accent-soft:#FFE1D9; --positive:#00A86B;
  --dark-bg:#111111; --on-dark:#FFFFFF; --on-dark-muted:#C4C4C4;
  --font-head:"Inter","PingFang SC","Noto Sans CJK SC","Microsoft YaHei",sans-serif;
  --font-body:"Inter","PingFang SC","Noto Sans CJK SC","Microsoft YaHei",sans-serif; --head-weight:800; }
```

Palette use: the coral-red (--dominant) is loud and takes big areas: full colour blocks,
half-bleed panels, giant numbers. Black (--accent) for type and secondary blocks. White space
is generous. Cover: full-bleed coral background with huge black or white title. Section
slides: "slide dark" (black) with a coral number.

Motif (every content slide): a solid coral block that bleeds off one edge — either a left
column 360–420 px wide holding the eyebrow and the key number, or a top band 150–200 px tall
holding the title in white.

Typography: titles 44–56 px, weight 800, tight letter-spacing (-0.01em); body 21–24 px;
big numbers 140–200 px weight 800; captions 18 px --muted.

Sample snippets:
- Left coral column with a giant white number and a one-line label; right side the title and
  2–3 short evidence lines.
- Three big tiles in a row, alternating coral / black / light grey, each one number + label.
